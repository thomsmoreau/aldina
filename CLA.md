Aldina's core is licensed under the GNU AGPL-3.0. Commercial licences that
exempt the AGPL's network clause are offered alongside it, and that revenue
funds the Project's development. Offering those licences requires the right to
sublicense every line in the tree — which is what clause 2 below grants.

to your Contributions and may use them anywhere else, under any terms you like.

## Scope: trivial contributions are exempt

This Agreement is not required for trivial contributions — a typo correction, a
one-line fix, a comment or documentation wording change, or anything else that
does not meet the threshold of copyrightable originality. Requiring paperwork
for those would be disproportionate. If you are unsure whether a change is
trivial, ask in the pull request.
